Research Agenda

Efficient large-model adaptation beyond centralized training.

My research goal is to make large-model adaptation practical in realistic distributed settings, where clients, networks, and tasks are heterogeneous and communication is often the dominant bottleneck.

01

Communication-Efficient Federated LLM Adaptation

Designing sparse upload scheduling, update selection, and aggregation mechanisms that preserve optimization signals under severe uplink budgets.

02

Federated Continual Learning for LLMs

Understanding and mitigating conflicts between new-task adaptation and historical knowledge preservation without relying on replay data.

03

Large Model Systems & Infrastructure

Building systems-oriented methods for post-training, distributed training/inference acceleration, and LLM quantization/optimization.

Research Projects

Selected research experience

A systems-oriented line of work on efficient model update transmission, conflict-aware selection, and decentralized learning.

2026–2027

Rethinking LoRA: Signal-Preserving Upload Scheduling for Federated LLM Adaptation

Signal-preserving sparse upload scheduling for federated LLM adaptation under tight uplink budgets.

  • Formulated low-budget FedLoRA as upload scheduling over structurally consistent A/B-pair effective updates.
  • Developed a signal-preserving allocation rule that estimates shared descent signal and heterogeneity noise.
  • Designed gap-aware client-module assignment using lightweight effective-update sketches and min-cost flow.
2025–2026

RieSelect: Conflict-Aware Layer Selection for Federated Continual Learning

Communication-efficient federated continual learning for LLMs under uplink budgets.

  • Identified the less-is-more effect, where denser uplink may transmit high-conflict layers and amplify forgetting.
  • Designed a Fisher-metric safe-basin mechanism to detect risky layer updates without replaying historical data.
  • Formulated sparse uplink as a utility-risk knapsack problem and implemented selection for T5-Large, LLaMA2-7B, and Qwen-14B.
2024–2025

FLM-TopK: Communication-Efficient Federated LLM Tuning

Gradient sparsification framework for communication-efficient federated large language model tuning.

  • Proposed intervalized TopK to reduce position-index overhead in sparse gradient transmission.
  • Modeled fine-tuning gradient distributions and optimized interval size and sparsification rate under a fixed communication budget.
  • Evaluated the framework on DistilBERT, RoBERTa, and LLaMA2-7B across multiple public datasets.
2022–2023

ADMM-dESN: Decentralized Federated Learning for Industrial IoT

Peer-to-peer federated learning framework based on deep echo state networks.

  • Built a decentralized federated learning framework for Industrial IoT without relying on a trusted central server.
  • Combined decentralized average consensus and ADMM to enable local optimization and distributed model aggregation.
  • Studied robustness, data heterogeneity, training time, and prediction performance across multiple benchmarks.
