2026 · Ongoing

FedSP-LoRA: Task-Aware Sparse Communication for Federated LoRA Adaptation Research in progress

When not every LoRA update fits within the uplink budget, how should a client decide which updates to transmit? This ongoing work investigates sparse communication at the level of complete LoRA module updates, with selection based on their estimated contribution to the learning objective rather than parameter magnitude alone.

  • Studies update-selection granularity and task-aware communication under per-client uplink budgets.
  • Investigates efficient local update scoring and budget-constrained selection.
  • Evaluates the effect of repeated sparse communication on downstream model adaptation.

Working title and research scope; not a publication or an accepted paper.

2025–2026

RieSelect: Conflict-Aware Layer Selection for Federated Continual Learning

Communication-efficient federated continual learning for LLMs under uplink budgets.

  • Identified the less-is-more effect, where denser uplink may transmit high-conflict layers and amplify forgetting.
  • Designed a Fisher-metric safe-basin mechanism to detect risky layer updates without replaying historical data.
  • Formulated sparse uplink as a utility-risk knapsack problem and implemented selection for T5-Large, LLaMA2-7B, and Qwen-14B.
2024–2025

FLM-TopK: Communication-Efficient Federated LLM Tuning

Gradient sparsification framework for communication-efficient federated large language model tuning.

  • Proposed intervalized TopK to reduce position-index overhead in sparse gradient transmission.
  • Modeled fine-tuning gradient distributions and optimized interval size and sparsification rate under a fixed communication budget.
  • Evaluated the framework on DistilBERT, RoBERTa, and LLaMA2-7B across multiple public datasets.
2022–2023

ADMM-dESN: Decentralized Federated Learning for Industrial IoT

Peer-to-peer federated learning framework based on deep echo state networks.

  • Built a decentralized federated learning framework for Industrial IoT without relying on a trusted central server.
  • Combined decentralized average consensus and ADMM to enable local optimization and distributed model aggregation.
  • Studied robustness, data heterogeneity, training time, and prediction performance across multiple benchmarks.
